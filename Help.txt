AsuraSwift - User Guide

Both computers must be on the same network. A phone hotspot works well:
have both machines join it. No internet or mobile data is used - the files
travel directly between the two computers.

To receive:
  1. Click RECEIVE.
  2. Choose the folder to save into.
  3. Wait. Your computer announces itself on the network automatically,
     and shows its address in case it is needed.

To send:
  1. Click SEND.
  2. Pick the receiving computer from the list. It appears on its own -
     there is no address, port or speed to type in. Start the receiver first.
  3. Choose any mix of files and folders, then press Send.

Folders keep their structure: subfolders and empty folders are recreated
exactly as they were at the destination.

If the device list stays empty, the network is probably blocking device
discovery (some public Wi-Fi and hotspots isolate clients from each other).
Use "Enter address manually" on the sender and type the address shown on
the receiving computer's waiting screen.
